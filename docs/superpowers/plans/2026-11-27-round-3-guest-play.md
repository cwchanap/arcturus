## Risks

Top risk is persisted Roulette/Craps snapshots overriding an isolated bankroll reset; verify session-owner mutation and reload. Other risks: settings still writing a Blackjack balance, stale auto-deal, UI showing guest reset during pending reveals, and auth recovery mistakenly using the guest path. The shared helpers and local guards should prevent duplication without creating an economy/state framework.
