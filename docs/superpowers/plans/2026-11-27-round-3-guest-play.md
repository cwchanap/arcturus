## Risks

Top risk is persisted Roulette/Craps snapshots overriding an isolated bankroll reset; verify session-owner mutation and reload. Other risks: settings still writing a Blackjack balance, stale auto-deal/controls, Baccarat resolution before reveal and Blackjack unsettled complete phase mistaken for safe idle, guest preview scripts intercepting header controls, and auth recovery mistakenly using the guest path. The shared helpers and local guards should prevent duplication without creating an economy/state framework.
